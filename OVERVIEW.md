A pet (Rottweiler or cat) in the sidebar footer that mirrors your agents. It sleeps when nothing runs, walks back and forth while agents work, and waves when an agent waits for a permission. Its label counts the agents it is watching.

Click the pet to see the working agents with their workspace and host, then click one to open it. It covers every connected host. Pick Rottweiler or cat at the top of that list; the choice is saved.

The plugin runs in the app only. Every 3 seconds it reads the agent and workspace lists of each online host and sends nothing anywhere else.
